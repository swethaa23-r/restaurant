The Beginning

How It All Started

Founded in 2010 by a small group of passionate culinary artists, Stackly was born out of a desire to create a dining space that feels both extraordinarily luxurious and warmly familiar.

What began as a 30-seat bistro has blossomed into a critically acclaimed fine dining establishment, yet our core mission remains unchanged: to bring joy to people through exceptional food.

Our Philosophy

"Nature dictates our menu, not the other way around."

We are deeply committed to sustainability and seasonality. By partnering exclusively with local organic farms, ethical fisheries, and artisan purveyors, we ensure that every ingredient on your plate is at its absolute peak of flavor and quality.

The Visionary

Chef Marcus Chen

Chef Marcus Chen grew up in the bustling markets of Taipei before moving to Paris to train under some of the world's most renowned culinary masters. His unique background allows him to blend precise European techniques with bold, vibrant Asian flavors.

"Cooking is a continuous conversation between the chef, the ingredients, and the guest. When you dine at Stackly, you are participating in that dialogue."
